# A plan for the day or the week

Tools: `my_work`, `my_notifications`, `work_summary`, `workload`, `update_task`, `plan_changes`, `apply_plan`.

1. `my_work`: overdue, today, this week, proposals waiting. `my_notifications`: unread first.
2. Week: `work_summary` with scope "me" for the week (slipped, due, blocked), and `workload` for capacity.
3. Day brief: three to five things for today, overdue and at-risk first, then due dates; one line each with why.
4. Week plan: day by day within capacity; say what does not fit and propose what to move, with new dates.
5. Moving dates: one task with `update_task`, several with one plan (op "edit_task" per task). Only after a yes.
6. Proposals: hand over to the proposals workflow of this skill.
