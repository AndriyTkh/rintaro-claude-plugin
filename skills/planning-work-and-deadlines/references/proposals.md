# Answering proposed tasks

Tools: `my_work`, `get_task`, `workload`, `accept_task`, `decline_task`, `add_comment`.

1. `my_work` lists the proposals waiting for the person; `get_task` gives the details and who proposed each.
2. Against `workload`, recommend accept or decline for each, with a one-line reason.
3. Do nothing until the person answers per proposal. Declining needs a reason the proposer will read: draft it
   kindly and let the person edit it, then `decline_task` (and `add_comment` if they want more said).
4. Accepted tasks: offer to place them in the week plan.
