# Upcoming deadlines

Tools: `search_tasks`, `list_grants`, `list_work_packages`, `get_deadline_rules`.

1. Range: what the person said, else the next 14 days. `search_tasks` with the due-from and due-to dates, all pages.
2. Group by grant (`list_grants`) and work package (`list_work_packages`). Dates set by a rule: `get_deadline_rules`
   explains them.
3. Overdue and at-risk first, then by date: code, title, assignee, due date.
4. End with the three items most likely to slip and who to talk to. Change nothing.
