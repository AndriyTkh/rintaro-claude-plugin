# Weekly status update

Tools: `work_summary`, `list_work_packages`, `search_tasks`, `get_task`.

1. `work_summary` for the period with scope "team" (or "me", or one work package with `wpId`): done, started,
   slipped, due and blocked.
2. `list_work_packages` to group by work package; `get_task` for a slipped or blocked task's latest comment when
   the reason matters.
3. Write, short and factual, grouped by work package:
   - done (highlights first);
   - in progress;
   - slipped, with the reason when a comment gives one;
   - blockers and who can unblock them;
   - next deadlines (the coming two weeks).
4. Give it to the person to send. Offer a shorter version for a chat message.
