# Grant progress

Tools: `list_grants`, `get_grant`, `list_work_packages`, `search_tasks`, `work_summary`.

1. `list_grants`, then `get_grant` for the one in question: periods, deliverables and milestones with dates.
2. For each deliverable and milestone: its tasks (`search_tasks`, `list_work_packages`), how many are done, what is
   late, and the next date.
3. `work_summary` for the current period to show momentum: done and slipped since the last update.
4. Write it per work package: on track, at risk (with why), late; then the next three dates. Change nothing.
